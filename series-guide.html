The Inspector Griffin Mysteries

A Modern Cyber-Noir Series

The Inspector Griffin Mysteries follow the unlikely partnership of Adam Griffin, the wealthy heir to a literary fortune, and Finn Williams, an elite ethical hacker recruited from MI5.

Blending the classic "whodunit" structure with high-stakes digital espionage, each book can be read as a standalone mystery, but reading them in order reveals the deepening bond between Adam and Finn as they navigate a world of corporate greed, cyber-crime, and murder.
