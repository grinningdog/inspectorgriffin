Adam Griffin

Skilled Amateur Detective

The wealthy grandson of one of the world's greatest mystery writers.
Adam Griffin inherited a fortune from his grandmother - probably the greatest crime writer of the last hundred years. He also inherited her instinct for investigating crime and scandal.

When Adam reconnected with his partner Finn Williams they found themselves embroiled in mystery and intrigue.

Robert Chester's Interview with Adam

Robert Chester: Adam, you seem to have had an unconventional upbringing. How was that?

Adam Griffin: Do you think it was unconventional? I think lots of people are brought up by a grandparent. Perhaps the only difference for me was that my parents were around, just not very well engaged.

RC: But your grandmother was obviously a huge influence on you while you were growing up.

AG: For sure, she taught me tolerance, patience and compassion for others but she also taught me independence and good discipline.

RC: As such a prolific writer, did she have time for you? Her life must have been hectic.

AG: More than time, and more than a grandmother really. It sounds like a hackneyed old line but she really was my best friend.

RC: Was she upset when you told her you're gay?

AG: Not at all. She always said she just wanted me to be happy. It helped that her best friend was gay and I suspect she had knew I was gay before I even had the words to tell her.

RC: You and Finn make quite the team. How do your different skill sets complement each other during an investigation?

AG: Finn has the most amazing skills. Sometimes astonishing. He is so logical and focused but sometimes it takes me to add the illogical, emotional elements. The parts we're all affected by.

RC: So, is one the brains and one the heart?

AG: Haha, not at all. I suspect we both contribute brains and heart. It's the ability to 'ping' off each other and fill in each other's gaps that makes us strong.

AG: Finn has the most amazing skills. Sometimes astonishing. He is so logical and focused but sometimes it takes me to add the illogical, emotional elements. The parts we're all affected by.'

RC: How do you see the future?

AG: With Finn. We're very lucky that we are financially and emotionally secure and I'd love to think that we'll be together forever.

RC: Solving crimes when you are pensioners?

AG: Well who knows what the future will bring? But whether it's lots of adventures or just snuggling up on the sofa, I'll be happy as long as I'm with Finn.
