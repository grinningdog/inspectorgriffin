About Robert Chester

Author of the Inspector Griffin Mysteries

Robert Chester is a contemporary British author specializing in high-stakes cyber thrillers and modern crime fiction. Blending the classic "whodunit" structure of Golden Age mysteries with the digital complexities of the 21st century, Chester creates worlds where financial logic and ethical hacking collide.

Best known for The Inspector Griffin Mysteries, Chester explores themes of legacy, identity, and the hidden dangers of our connected world. His protagonists, the wealthy amateur sleuth Adam Griffin and the elite MI5-trained hacker Finn Williams, have captivated readers looking for diverse, LGBTQ+ representation in the mystery genre.

When not writing, Robert can be found researching the latest cybersecurity trends or exploring the historic streets of London that often serve as the backdrop for his novels.
