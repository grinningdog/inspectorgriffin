Robby Dorset

Member of the Aristocracy and the British establishment

Robby Dorset has known Adam from Adam's birth and was the best friend of his grandmother. Always full of surprises, he is a man who knows everyone. He also knows all their secrets and loves mystery and intrigue

Robert Chester's Interview with Robby

Robert Chester: Can we start with your name Robby because I believe you have a different title?

Robby Dorset: Oh my dear boy... it's somewhat tedious because nobody cares about that stuff any more but, yes, my official name is Robert Louis Deauville- French

RC: But you're also His Grace, the 11th Duke of Dorset aren't you?

RD: I am indeed, with a huge estate in the country that costs a fortune to maintain.

RC: Is it true you were asked to leave school because of bad behaviour?

RD: The tragedy of my life is that nearly all the stories you will hear about me are true. As soon as I left school I created my own little maelstrom in tthe very centre of high society. No fiction could capture the drama, and the scandal, the really happened.

RC: How do you know Adam and Finn?

RD: I was very fortunate to be a very good friend of Adam's grandmother, Elspeth Potter, so I have know Adam since before he was born. Obviously I met Finn through Adam but I love him like a second son.

RC: I've heard you described as Adam's Godfather.

RD: Well, there was no official service but I have always adopted that role. Especially when he needed protection from his own parents.

RC: Were his parents difficult?

RD: Difficult? No particularly but more... self obsessed. His father could become troublesome when drunk but mainly he was a placid, quiet sort of alcoholic.

RC: Is that why Adam's hardly drinks?

RD: Oh really Mr Chester. Surely you don't expect me to answer that. Why not ask me how many members of the Royal Family I've bedded?

RC: Was it hard being gay in the 1950s and 60s?

RD: Yes and no. I realise that I was, I am, in a privileged position. If you were less privileged then I think your life could be very hard. In high society there was some acceptance as long as one didn't speak of such things?

RC: How do you view the future for yourself as well as Adam and Finn?

RD: For me? At 86 the days are obviously numbered but, while I am fit and able, I will continue to enjoy life. I hope to emulate my friend Elspeth and reach 100 years old. At that point I anticipate being even more scandalous.

RC: And Adam and Finn?

RD: Rock solid. They are lucky to have found each other so young so that they will have many happy years together. And I have told them that I will come back and haunt them both if they dare to separate.

RC: Last question: just how many members of the Royal Family have you slept with?

RD: I am sworn to secrecy and, as anyone will tell you, I am not a gossip.
