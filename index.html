The Inspector Griffin Mysteries

When Adam Griffin meets Finn Williams, the world changes.

Adam Griffin is the sole heir to his grandmother - the legendary writer of the Inspector Griffin novels. Finn Williams is a master hacker, recruited from school by the British Intelligence Service MI5 and now probably the best ethical hacker in the world.

When they reunite, their relationship sparks but they are surrounded by intrigue and mystery.
